About the Foundation

Building trust through clear public frameworks.

Union Foundation publishes governance materials for certificate, licensing, registration, and trust-related services. Our focus is clarity: what a process covers, how it is reviewed, and where to find the governing documents.

Our purpose

Make authority understandable.

Authority processes can affect identity, access, and organizational records. We aim to make the applicable policies and responsibilities easier to discover and review through a structured public document library.

Our approach

Policy, process, and accountability.

Published materials describe intended procedures, decision criteria, review paths, and contact channels. Each framework is presented as governance information and should be read alongside the specific terms that apply to a service.
