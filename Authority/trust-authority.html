Trust Authority

Framework for transparent governance and responsible oversight of trust-related services.

Scope & process

Trust depends on visible accountability

The Trust Authority framework brings together the governance principles that support the Foundation's authority programs. It describes expectations for documented policy, appropriate review, public disclosure, and response to reported concerns.

Governance principles

  • Publish material policies and explain the scope of each authority role.
  • Use documented criteria and retain appropriate records of formal decisions.
  • Provide channels for questions, correction requests, and reports of suspected issues.
  • Review reported incidents and communicate material updates when appropriate.
Trust is not a certification claim.

This page describes governance intent and does not represent external accreditation, regulatory approval, or a guarantee of any service's suitability.
