Registration Authority

Framework for receiving, validating, and maintaining program registration records.

Scope & process

Accurate records, accountable handling

The Registration Authority framework covers registration intake and the administrative review of information submitted for a Union Foundation program. It describes record handling at a high level; any program-specific verification requirements are defined by the applicable terms and procedures.

Core stages

  • Receive registration details and identify the applicable program requirements.
  • Check submissions for completeness and perform any required validation.
  • Record a registration outcome and provide a route to address missing or inaccurate information.
  • Review correction and access requests in accordance with applicable policy and law.
Submit only what is required.

Do not send sensitive personal information unless a specific official process requests it and provides an appropriate secure channel.

Related information

For questions about how information is handled, review the Privacy Notice or contact the Foundation.
