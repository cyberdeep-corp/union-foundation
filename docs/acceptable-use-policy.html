Legal / responsible use

Acceptable Use Policy

Expectations for responsible use of Union Foundation websites and services.

Users must use the website and any related service lawfully and in a manner that respects the security, availability, and integrity of the service and the rights of others.

Prohibited activity

  • Attempting unauthorized access or circumventing access controls.
  • Disrupting service availability or probing systems without authorization.
  • Submitting malicious content, false information, or another person's confidential information without authority.
  • Impersonating an authority, applicant, organization, or rights holder.
  • Using site content to mislead others about a certificate, license, registration, or endorsement.

Reporting a concern

We may review suspected misuse and take reasonable steps consistent with applicable terms and law. Report a concern through the report page or contact us.

This policy does not grant permission to access any system or resource beyond what is expressly authorized.
