Governance Disclosure

How the Foundation describes the scope and accountability principles of its authority framework.

Authority scope

Union Foundation publishes materials relating to certificate, license, registration, and trust authority frameworks. Each public framework is intended to identify the relevant role, process overview, and links to applicable policy documents.

Accountability principles

  • Make material public policies accessible and understandable.
  • Describe decisions and review processes in accordance with applicable service terms.
  • Maintain appropriate records and provide channels for questions and reports.
  • Clarify limits and avoid presenting general guidance as accreditation or a guarantee.
