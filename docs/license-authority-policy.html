License Authority Policy

Principles for consistent review, communication, and oversight of license decisions.

The Foundation's License Authority uses the applicable published eligibility criteria and license terms to guide review. Program terms define the rights granted, any applicable fees, duration, and conditions; this general policy does not create a license.

Application and decision

  • Identify the license and the relevant criteria before review.
  • Assess information submitted for the stated purpose and request clarification when needed.
  • Record the outcome and communicate any conditions, limitations, or next steps.

Renewal and review

Renewal, amendment, complaint, and review requests are handled under the terms applicable to the license. A request does not guarantee a particular outcome.

Records and conflicts

Material decisions should be documented for appropriate accountability. If this policy summary conflicts with a specific license, service agreement, or applicable law, consult the specific terms and seek qualified advice as appropriate.
