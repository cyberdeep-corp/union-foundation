Registration Authority Policy

Principles for handling program registration submissions and maintaining accurate records.

This policy establishes a general framework for receiving and reviewing registration information submitted to a Union Foundation program. Program-specific requirements and notices describe the precise information required and how it is used.

Review and recordkeeping

  • Collect only information needed for the stated registration purpose.
  • Review submissions for completeness and perform applicable validation checks.
  • Record outcomes and material changes using appropriate access controls.
  • Provide a reasonable channel to request correction of inaccurate records.
  • Handle records in line with applicable retention requirements and the Privacy Notice.
Information handling

Do not send sensitive information through ordinary email or an unsecured form unless specifically requested through a suitable process.
