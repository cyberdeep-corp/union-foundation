Privacy Notice

How to think about information shared through this public website and related inquiries.

Static site notice

This website is published as static pages. The report form is not connected to a submission service and should not be used to send personal or confidential information.

Information you choose to provide

If you contact the Foundation by email, the information in your message and contact details may be used to respond, address the inquiry, and maintain appropriate records. Avoid including sensitive personal information unless it is necessary and a secure channel has been provided.

Website operations

The hosting provider may process technical request data, such as browser and server logs, to deliver and protect the website. The Foundation does not describe or control the provider's specific retention or processing practices on this page; consult the provider's current privacy information.

Sharing, retention, and choices

Information may be retained or disclosed as needed to respond, protect the site and services, meet legal obligations, or resolve a dispute. Applicable law may give you rights concerning personal information. Contact the Foundation to ask a question or make a request; identity and scope may need to be verified.

Updates

This notice may be updated as site operations change. For privacy inquiries, use the contact details on the Contact page.

This notice is general information, not a representation about a specific hosting provider's data practices.
